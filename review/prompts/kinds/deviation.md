A deviation is where the change goes against a written rule. The change may be wrong, the rule may lack an exception, or the convention may have moved on. The diff can't tell which, so the user decides. Say whether the rule's doc was routed, meaning its `when` matches the work. Ask the user whether to fix the change, or to change the rule by sharpening its wording, adding the exception the change shows, or replacing it. When the rule's doc wasn't routed, its `when` may also need fixing, whichever way the rest goes.
